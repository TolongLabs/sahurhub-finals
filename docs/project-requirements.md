## 1. Eligibility

- **Teams:** solo or up to **three (3)** members. No prior experience required. **One submission per team or individual.**
- **Cost:** free. Prelims are self-serve — participants provide their own Qwen model access (§4).
- **Registration:** via Luma; closed 13 July 2026, 23:59 MYT (200+ registered at publication).

## 2. The Brief

> Build the **most deliberately useless product that nonetheless functions**.

1. **Choose a deliberately useless problem.** The more absurd the premise, the stronger the result. Official examples: a toaster that toasts only one specific shape; an AI that argues with houseplants; a calendar that schedules naps.
2. **Constraints drive creativity.** A narrow, absurd brief leaves more room for invention.
3. **The build must be functional.** A slide deck or concept description is not a submission. A Qwen model must perform a genuine task within the build, however pointless that task may be (§4).
4. **Entries must remain in good taste.** Shock value is not a substitute for creativity (§5).

## 3. Submission Requirements (Prelims)

The preliminaries are **judged entirely on the poster**. Requirements are assessed as written; ambiguity is resolved against this section.

| Requirement         | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Format**          | One (1) square (1:1) poster at **1080 × 1080 pixels**, **PNG or JPG**. The poster is the sole submission artifact for the preliminaries.                                                  |
| **Purpose**         | A showcase/advertisement for the product: what the build is and why it is gloriously useless. Full creative freedom, subject to the Content Rules (§5) and the Qwen logo requirement.     |
| **Qwen logo**       | Must visibly include the Qwen logo or wordmark in legible form — **not altered, obscured, or used in a misleading way**. Use either official asset (white or coloured), unaltered.        |

| **Voting**          | Submission and voting run together 14–19 July. Voting opens **14 July, 21:00 MYT**; both close **19 July, 23:59 MYT**. Voting runs through a Google Form on the Submission & Voting page. |

A pitch line on the poster is optional (per FAQ).

## 4. Qwen Integration (Mandatory)

A Qwen model must perform **genuine work** within the build — a condition of entry, not optional.

- Use a Qwen model in **text, vision, audio, or code**. "Intended but not completed" is not acceptable; the integration must be **demonstrable**.
- **No API credits are provided for the preliminaries.** Access the models via your own **Qwen Cloud** or **ModelScope** account; either satisfies the requirement.
- **State which specific Qwen model powers your build.** A vague claim of AI usage is not a valid integration. The Qwen logo on the poster is the minimum visible proof.
- The **working integration is verified at the Physical Final** (live demo), not during prelims — prelims are judged on the poster alone.

## 6. Prelim Judging

A shortlist advances to the Physical Final. **Shortlisting is driven by community votes (criterion 4)** — the shortlist is drawn from the most-voted entries, checked against criteria 1–3.

| No. | Criterion                       | Description                                                                                                                                     |
| --- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Poster submission               | Compliant 1080×1080 poster received before the deadline. **Missing logo, wrong dimensions, or late submission fails this criterion.**           |
| 2   | Qwen integration (poster claim) | Poster **names a specific Qwen model** and shows the Qwen logo. Poster-level claim only; the working integration is verified live at the Final. |
| 3   | Guideline compliance            | Satisfies the Content Rules (§5).                                                                                                               |
| 4   | Voting                          | Google Form votes determine shortlisting; each person may vote once. More votes → higher chance of advancing.                                   |

## 7. Finals Judging

Shortlisted entries present a **working prototype on-site**, scored by the judging panel against a weighted rubric (100 points). **The rubric, not community votes, determines the awards.**

> **Build on-site rule:** Finalists have **two (2) hours** at the Physical Final to build their prototype. **Pre-built work brought to the venue is not permitted; an entry substantially built in advance will be disqualified.** Hardware projects are welcome but must be assembled/wired during the build window, not prepared beforehand.

| No. | Criterion                  | Weight     | Description                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | -------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Creativity & Originality   | **50 pts** | The "useless but works" factor — heaviest-weighted. Rewards a novel, absurd premise and an unexpected solution, not polish for its own sake. |
| 2   | Presentation & Showmanship | **30 pts** | Clarity of the demo and commitment to the premise: energy, pacing, strength of the bit.                                                      |
| 3   | Qwen Integration Depth     | **20 pts** | How centrally and effectively a Qwen model powers the build. Trivial model work scores lower than model-core function.                       |

**Tiebreakers (in order):** higher Creativity & Originality → higher Presentation & Showmanship → higher Qwen Integration Depth → Head Judge decides.

## Strategic Implications for SahurHub

Derived from the rules, not stated in them:

1. **The prelim game is a popularity contest with compliance gates.** Votes decide the shortlist; the poster only needs to pass criteria 1–3. Poster quality + vote mobilization matter more than code quality before 19 July.
2. **The prototype this week serves two purposes only:** making the poster's Qwen-model claim true (it must be demonstrable at finals) and rehearsing a build that can be **redone from scratch in 2 hours on-site**.
3. **Weight effort like the finals rubric:** absurdity of premise (50) > demo showmanship (30) > integration depth (20). Tiebreakers cascade the same way.
4. **Pin the exact Qwen model early** — the poster must name it verbatim.
5. **Deadline discipline:** poster in before 19 July 23:59 MYT with correct dimensions and an unaltered logo — any of those three failing disqualifies the entry from shortlisting regardless of votes.
